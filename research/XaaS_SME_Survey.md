# XaaS SME export survey — full instrument (Google Forms ready)

**Date:** 2026-06-15
**Length target:** 5–8 minutes. **Sample:** non-probability (analyse descriptively only).
**Setup notes for Google Forms are in [brackets].** Each question marks its type, whether it's
required, and any branching.

**Ordering principle (state in methodology):** firm profile and pain questions come *before* the
product is described, so the concept does not prime how people rate their problems
("discovery before concept").

## Section 5 — Pricing  [Van Westendorp — word these exactly]

**[Section description — anchor the respondent first:]**
> Imagine the tool described above were offered as a monthly subscription for your company (in euros
> per month). Please answer the next four questions with a price in euros. There are no wrong
> answers — your instinct is what matters.

**[All four: Short answer · required · response validation = Number · prefix/suffix "€ / month".
Keep them in THIS order.]**

**Q16. (Too cheap)** At what monthly price would you think the tool is *so cheap* that you'd doubt
its quality or trustworthiness?

**Q17. (Bargain)** At what monthly price would you consider the tool *a bargain* — great value for
the money?

**Q18. (Getting expensive)** At what monthly price would the tool *start to feel expensive* — not
out of the question, but you'd have to think carefully before subscribing?

**Q19. (Too expensive)** At what monthly price would the tool be *so expensive* that you would not
consider subscribing at all?

**[Analysis note — not shown to respondents:** plot the four cumulative curves. The acceptable price
range runs from the **Point of Marginal Cheapness** (intersection of "too cheap" and "getting
expensive") to the **Point of Marginal Expensiveness** (intersection of "bargain" and "too
expensive"). The **Optimal Price Point** is where "too cheap" and "too expensive" cross. Report the
range, not a single number. Drop respondents whose answers aren't monotonic, e.g. bargain ≥ too
expensive.]**

## Deployment & analysis checklist

- **Branching:** Q1 "No" and Q2 "Neither" both route to the "Not eligible" section. In Forms: open
  each of those questions' ⋮ menu → "Go to section based on answer."
- **Required vs optional:** screening, profile, pain, spend, interest, and the four price questions
  are required; open-text and contact are optional (forcing open-text lowers completion).
- **Numbers:** on Q16–Q19, set Response validation → Number, so the price data is clean to analyse.
- **Anonymity:** keep Q20 (email) in its own section and, in Settings, do **not** collect email
  addresses automatically — that preserves the "anonymous" claim.
- **Pilot the form** on 2–3 people first to catch confusing wording before sending widely.
- **Reporting caveat (put in the write-up):** convenience sample → report distributions and the
  Van Westendorp range descriptively; do **not** present percentages as representative of all LT/EU
  SME exporters.
